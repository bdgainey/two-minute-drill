how our game can quickly fix typos

i found an easy way that apps use to assume what word you mean

1. check the word as typed
If someone types "elephant" and it’s on our list it’s accepted right away

2. Look for close spellings
Before the game starts we make a lookup table. For each word we save
versions with one letter removed. For example, if we drop the "n" from
"elephant", we get "elephat". We save that "elephat" points to "elephant".

Now say someone types "elephnat". We try dropping one letter at a time.
Dropping the "n" also gives us "elephat". The table points us to "elephant".
We check the two words and see that two letters were swapped.

3. Fix clear typos and ask when it’s a close call.
If there’s one clear match, we can fix it and show the corrected word.
If a few words are close, we can show them and let the player pick. (I prefer not)
We can put the closest spellings first and use common words to break ties.
If nothing is close, we ask the player to try again.

This method is called SymSpell. It’s fast because we build the table once.
During play, we check a few short spellings and compare the matches.
There’s no need for an AI call or a scan of the full word list.

The spell check just helps us settle on a word. Once we have that word,
we can just send it to our game’s ranking system.
